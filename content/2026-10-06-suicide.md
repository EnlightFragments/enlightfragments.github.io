title: Suicide
publishDate: 2026-10-06T00:00:00-00:00
---

Fanatical thinking often leads to suicide.

Essentially, fanatical thinking is suicide, because it turns you into a confident, predictable, dead machine.

Critically thinking person would necessarily doubt at least the following:

- What life is?
- How does it work?
- What's really going on?
- What really comes after death?
- Is life worse than nothing?
- What does "worse" even mean and how do you measure it?

Those who are unfortunate enough to be unable to find simple answers to these questions are doomed to remain trapped in the struggle of existence.
