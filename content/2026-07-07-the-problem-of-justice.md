---
title: The Problem of Justice
publishDate: 2026-07-07T00:00:00-00:00
---

There are events that happen which are considered to be unjust. Guided by emotions, it
feels counterintuitive, but the root of the problem lies in the absense of a
universal definition for justice. The mere possibility of injustice happening
suggests that the world does not operate as we would like it to.
